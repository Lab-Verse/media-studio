# Media production tools and skills research

Checked 16 November 2026. Scope is local-first media production for YouTube and social channels, with Urdu as the first proposed language to qualify. Recommendations weigh proof on the user's Spark, maintainability, creative control, editability, rights and quality at realistic throughput. Vendor launch and support statements establish feature availability. On-device evidence and creative acceptance require the local test matrix.

## Picture, speech and sound

| Option | Production decision and uncertainty |
| --- | --- |
| Wan 2.2 A14B scaled-FP8 and current film graph | Default for the first generated shots. Upstream Wan 2.2 is Apache 2.0. Capture the actual repack/checkpoints and companion assets in license review. In the existing project, T2V and I2V rendered, and an eight-step, 80-frame, 1280×720/16 fps A14B film smoke render took about 19.5 minutes. The 5B vs A14B shot comparison used different steps and cadence. Use neither datapoint as proof of longer renders or a quality ranking. [Wan upstream](https://github.com/Wan-Video/Wan2.2) · [NVIDIA Spark ComfyUI playbook](https://github.com/NVIDIA/dgx-spark-playbooks/blob/main/nvidia/playbook-comfyui/README.md) |

NVIDIA identifies Spark optimization. These claims do not establish quality, generated Urdu fidelity or speed on this unit. Compare I2V first and use the same approved recorded dialogue for audio-driven auditions. The LTX-2.x Community License applies to the weights, with use restrictions and a paid commercial-use requirement for entities at or above the defined USD 10 million revenue threshold. Check the entity definition and gated download terms. [Comfy release](https://blog.comfy.org/p/ltx-25-day-0-support-in-comfyui) · [NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/local-ai-open-source-models-agents-nemotron/) · [LTX model card](https://huggingface.co/Lightricks/LTX-2.5) · [Weights terms](https://github.com/Lightricks/LTX-2/blob/main/LICENSE-2_x) |
| MiniMax H3 | Monitor as a secondary challenger for audiovisual generation. Local Comfy support exists. Exact checkpoint, territory, commercial license and deployment size need review before testing or release. Keep it out of the first pilot. [MiniMax](https://design.minimax.io/h3) · [Comfy integration](https://blog.comfy.org/p/minimax-h3-day-0-support-in-comfyui) |
| Urdu recording and ASR | Use native cast or in-house voices as the release reference and preserve dry tracks. Faster-Whisper can generate word timestamps for drafts, which must be aligned with heard dialogue and corrected. In existing Spark records it produced timestamped transcripts for 31 LHM sources. A private vertical cut passed decode but still needed final host framing and first-word playback. Use that as a specific QC checkpoint. [Faster-Whisper](https://github.com/SYSTRAN/faster-whisper) · [Whisper languages](https://github.com/openai/whisper/blob/main/whisper/tokenizer.py) |
| Urdu speech synthesis | Pilot a single-speaker Matcha Urdu ONNX audition only after model, training data, vocoder and voice-use review. The model card lists Apache 2.0, which does not establish rights in all components or training inputs. OmniVoice's current pretrained model card is CC BY-NC, so skip it for commercial release. An opt-in cloud audition can compare Pakistan Urdu voices if acceptable under the production's privacy and commercial terms. No local synthetic voice is preapproved by this plan. [Matcha Urdu](https://huggingface.co/AhsanTalal/urdu-matcha-tts) · [OmniVoice model card](https://huggingface.co/k2-fsa/OmniVoice) · [Azure Speech language support](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-services/speech-service/language-support?tabs=text-to-speech) |
| Music and SFX | Start with commissioned or in-house recordings, Foley, room tone and separately cleared tracks with retained rights evidence. Stable Audio 3 is an optional local audition for effects/music, subject to the exact model and commercial-use registration/terms. Check usage by channel and track. [Stable Audio licensing](https://kb.stability.ai/knowledge-base/stable-audio-commerical-and-usage-licensing) · [YouTube Audio Library](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15577610) |
| Assembly and finishing | Fountain for Git-friendly scripts, Resolve for a human-editable timeline/color/Fairlight workflow and FFmpeg for deterministic delivery/decode/loudness. Remotion skills are useful for reusable graphic layers and standardized cutdowns, subject to its license. [Fountain](https://fountain.io/syntax/) · [Resolve training](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve/training) · [OpenTimelineIO](https://opentimelineio.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) · [FFmpeg loudnorm](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#loudnorm) · [Remotion terms](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/license) |

## Distribution and rights

- **YouTube.** Record AI use for realistic AI-generated or meaningfully altered content per the current upload guidance. Monetization review rewards original and authentic content and has distinct inauthentic and reused-content policies. Standard-channel square or vertical Shorts can run up to three minutes. A Short longer than one minute with an active copyright claim is blocked globally. Evaluate eligibility and upload policy again for a named release. [YouTube AI use](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491) · [Monetization](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392) · [Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877)
- **Reels and TikTok.** Deliver a dedicated vertical cut with readable captions and licensed music and check current in-app frame/caption placement. TikTok requires labeling realistic AI-generated imagery, audio or video. Check each platform's current upload surface and terms before committing a public release or assuming a track license transfers. [Meta AI labels](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/04/metas-approach-to-labeling-ai-generated-content-and-manipulated-media/) · [TikTok AI labels](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/ai-generated-content)
- **Production rights.** Separate terms for script or adaptation, recording, voice/likeness, model, source footage, music/samples and platform. Capture written terms for intended territory, length of use, channel, commercial rights and whether a derivative or synthetic use is included. Where an adaptation or synthetic likeness is ambiguous, secure clearance before committing the final release. An original fictional pilot avoids dependence on a literary or film adaptation license.

Highest remaining uncertainties are sustained GPU throughput, consistent characters through actor coverage and multiple episodes, native Urdu delivery, recoverable packaging and channel performance. The pilot and benchmark in the [implementation plan](../IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md) specify the evidence required for each decision.
