---
name: studio-creative-development
description: Develop and test original concepts hooks and visual or sound treatments for social videos audio shows dramas and seasons.
---

- Extract the audience promise, intended feeling, medium, culture and practical production limits. Use adjacent work to identify recognizable conventions and an original point of view. Treat references as inspiration and record any inputs that need permission.
- Explore two or three materially different directions when the brief is open. Vary premise or point of view, opening beat, reveal, motif, pacing or sonic perspective. Write the opening 5–10 seconds or first exchange for each. Explain how each pays off the promise.
- Recommend one direction with a working logline, audience reason, sample look and sound, memorable moment, and the biggest feasibility risk. Offer a fallback that preserves the dramatic intent. For episodic work, propose recurring motifs and variety across episodes.
- Test the most uncertain idea cheaply through a table read, storyboard, scratch audio or a 10–15 second animatic. Compare originality, emotional clarity, cultural and language fit, and cost. Ask the producer to record the selected direction and its downstream implications.
- Hand the treatment to writer, director, art and sound. At rough cut, suggest timestamped alternatives for stale beats or clarity issues within the agreed revision budget.
