---
name: studio-sound-and-captions
description: Direct speech or dubbing, edit dialogue and effects, transcribe, mix stems and check captions for English, Urdu or other specified languages.
---

- Freeze a pronunciation and cue sheet. Capture dry dialogue and room tone or use a rights-cleared voice. Audition against native recorded dialogue, including names, emotional turns, code-switching and sibilants.
- Cut dialogue first, align final words to picture, add ambience, effects and licensed music on distinct tracks. Preserve 48 kHz source/stems for editing and log loudness and peak measurements.
- Draft timestamps in Faster-Whisper if available, then compare against the approved script and actual audio. Preserve readable line breaks. Test Urdu RTL shaping, font fallback, punctuation, phone safe areas and the first audible word.
- Deliver the approved mix, dry dialogue/music/effects stems, language sidecar SRT or VTT and caption proof. Include exact version and defects.
