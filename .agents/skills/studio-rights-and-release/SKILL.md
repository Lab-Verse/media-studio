---
name: studio-rights-and-release
description: Check rights, consent, provenance and platform disclosures for original videos, licensed clips, music, footage, dubbing and generated media.
---

- For every material asset record the source/revision, rights evidence, authorized uses, platform, territory and term. Distinguish model code, weights, outputs and third-party inputs. Keep evidence for voice, likeness and confidential releases in restricted storage.
- Recheck current platform rules for AI/altered media, music claims and reused or inauthentic content. Use evidence from docs/research/MARKET_RESEARCH.md as a dated starting point. Apply the intended platform's upload settings when authorized.
- Prepare a release manifest and check the approved exact version, channel, disclosures and public assets. Review voice/likeness releases and any new rights issue before sharing the final. A private review file can exist locally without upload.
