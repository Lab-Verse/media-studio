---
name: studio-clip-production
description: Turn rights-cleared long video or podcast footage into coherent Shorts, Reels and audio excerpts with verified source timecodes and speaker framing.
---

- Establish source record and rights before selecting excerpts. Use timestamped transcript plus actual playback to find self-contained opens and complete answers. Keep the source timestamps and verify them against the source video.
- Make intent and scene change explicit. Default to a single episode per excerpt when source context matters. Add cross-source edits or multispeaker grids when the creative brief requires them and the cut remains intelligible.
- Reframe for 9:16 with face tracking or manual keyframes. Verify the opening speaker and words, cadence, captions, cuts and audio via actual playback. Export distinct assets for platforms when caption placement, length or music rights differ.
