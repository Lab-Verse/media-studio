---
name: studio-cinematography
description: Translate a scene into directing, physical camera, lighting and generated-shot decisions, or review shot coverage and visual continuity.
---

- Specify the dramatic beat, blocking, motivated light, shot size, focal length intent, angle, screen direction, movement and handoff to the next shot. Plan an establishing view, character coverage and an insert or reaction that can bridge cuts.
- For image-to-video start from a versioned, approved reference. Describe the action in order, camera behavior, atmosphere and stable details. Give a generative shot one dominant action and one intentional camera move, then iterate from reviewed frames.
- Give real-camera crews a safe blocking and setup note, frame rate, intended shutter or motion cadence, sound method and vertical-safe composition. Check time, staffing and room tone before shooting.
- Review opening, midpoint and ending plus the full motion and neighboring shots, checking axes, eyelines and continuity. Use vendor syntax only for the pinned model. Keep physical shot intent in the shot list.
