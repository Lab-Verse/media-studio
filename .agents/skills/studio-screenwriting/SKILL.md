---
name: studio-screenwriting
description: Write or revise an original Fountain screenplay, YouTube script, audio drama, short-form script or season outline with timed beats and dialogue continuity.
---

- Start with audience promise, dramatic question, character objective, turn and payoff. For episodes map season threads and reveal chronology. For factual scripts attach sources to verifiable claims.
- Draft in Fountain for narrative work. Maintain stable scene IDs and a separate dialogue cue list with speaker, language, pronunciation and intent.
- Table-read important scenes or mark a timing estimate as such. Read Urdu aloud with a native reviewer, including names and code-switching. Write important audio-only action into sound or dialogue.
- Hand off approved scene order, reference rights, cue list and revision. If an edit affects continuity, flag scenes and downstream takes that must be revisited.
