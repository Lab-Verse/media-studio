- Keep editable timelines, source timecodes/take IDs and selectors. Lock picture before final mix and caption pass. Use Resolve for scene editing and FFmpeg for repeatable transcodes and validation. Use Remotion only for reusable graphics or simple assemblies when licensed.
- Build separate 16:9 and 9:16 timelines or planned conform where required. Check face framing, first spoken word, visible caption areas, color tags, clip joins and intelligible dialogue at playback speed.
- Export the target's approved master, review encode and required text/audio assets. Probe codec, dimensions, duration and streams, decode the complete export, record SHA-256, and save a timestamped QC report.
