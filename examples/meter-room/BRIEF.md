# The Meter Room

Planning rehearsal for a fictional Urdu micro-drama. No people, voices, images, music or existing footage are attached. The original scenario and sample lines are written for this repository.

- **Audience** Urdu-speaking viewers of short narrative films.
- **Intent** A building caretaker faces a choice when the hallway lights come back and his neighbor asks where he has been.
- **Runtime** 24 seconds of planned coverage. A director rehearsal will establish actual pace.
- **Review** Read-only planning response. Check screen direction and alternate vertical coverage, then identify unresolved language, asset and timing decisions.
- **Next evidence** Record temporary dialogue, stage a rough timed animatic, and get feedback from a native Urdu listener before making a production commitment.
